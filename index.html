FAQs.

Will there be food?

Yes! We’ll provide three meals during the event. Please let the organisers know about any dietary requirements.

What does each track involve?

We’ll reveal the specific problem statements for each track at the event.

Is there a non-technical track?

Yes. Our Creative track is specifically for non-technical students.

What are the team sizes?

Teams must have between 3 and 5 people.

What if I don’t have a team?

No worries! You’ll have plenty of opportunities to meet people and find teammates. We’ll also run team-matching sessions to help :)
